Joints, Camera, Action! Co-Generating 3D Motion and Video for Arbitrary Rigs

Scene-aware mesh animation

Given a rigged asset, a 3D scene, an initial position, and a text prompt, our model jointly generates video and 3D motion consistent with the surrounding environment. These examples correspond to Section 4.2 of the paper.

Keyframe-Controlled Motion

As described in Section 4.4, given a text prompt, a static rig, and RGB keyframes, our model jointly generates video and 3D motion guided by the keyframes.

Video-to-Motion

As described in Section 4.4, our model estimates 3D motion for a supplied rig by keeping the input video clean and denoising only the motion, without requiring the first-frame pose.

Motion-to-Video

As described in Section 4.4, our model generates video from input 3D motion and a text prompt, with or without an initial image.

Training Datasets

We train on this heterogeneous mixture to expose the model to diverse scenes, appearances, motions, and skeletal topologies.

OiV- Objaverse in Video

We construct OiV, a dataset of 110,109 video–motion pairs, by rendering animated Objaverse assets and using a generative video editor to place them in VLM-selected environments while retaining their known 3D motion as ground truth.

OpenVid

Human motion data is collected by combining SAM-Body-4D for body motion and Dyn-HAMR for hand motion.

OpenVid contains 57,095 video–motion pairs.

HAA500

Human motion data is collected by combining SAM-Body-4D for body motion and Dyn-HAMR for hand motion.

HAA500 contains 3,449 video–motion pairs.

Human Data Pretraining

HumanML3D

We use the official HumanML3D dataset of human motions and text descriptions for pretraining.

BONES-SEED Augmented

100,000 motion–text samples from BONES-SEED, with augmented skeletons to increase skeletal diversity.
